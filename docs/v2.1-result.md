## What v2.1 was supposed to do

One command, `PYTHONPATH=src python -m kanon.v2 --seed 0`, both searches the schema and trains the networks. Before v2.1 the exercise in that command said a constant `(abstain f0)` and the AdamW step did not run.

v2.1 ties the utterance and the step to the same process:

- at epsilon = 1 the sampler speaks a legal composition of the two cards in the window, and the network is not called
- below 1 the network starts from the 176-token observation prefix
- a group hit calls AdamW on seeds 0-3, and solo only on seed 5
- seed 4 is frozen: a sampler attempt, no step
- there is no fifth network
- a second launch with `resume.pt` and `canon/snapshot.json` loads the frontier and the shelf

The plan has workshop levels 4-6, and the v2.1 prompts said not to build them yet. The run reached level 4 with an empty task and the steps stopped. Tactic tasks were added: the window holds the claim, the speaker utters a tactic, and the kernel grades it. Only from that moment does the workshop on levels 4-6 actually train.

## Shelf

Families that came down: constant 0, identity, constant 1, successor, addition, subtraction, parity.

Three shared facts, all shallow:

- E: `(zero)` equals `(call e4 (zero) (zero))`, that is zero and adding zero to zero
- W: `(call e0 (zero))` rewrites to `(zero)`, that is the constant zero
- C: the same equality as E, as a short chain

Multiplication, comparison, bounded sum, gcd, and exponent are not on the shelf. There is no file of 50 tasks. The `luka` column in the metrics is empty for the whole run. The package columns for addition, multiplication, and the first fact E also stayed empty, even though addition is on the shelf: the milestone counter did not store the package index.

## Learning

Seeds 0, 1, 2, 3, and solo (5) differ from a fresh `build_net`. Seed 4 is bitwise fresh. That is the learning condition from prompt 2, and it was met.

Accuracy in the metrics is a hit on the latest utterances, not an arithmetic score on unseen tasks. The frozen column is a sampler attempt at epsilon = 1, with no step. It is the baseline.

## Conclusion

Four networks learned, on this shelf, to utter a tactic the kernel accepts clearly more often than the network whose weights do not move. That is the v2.1 result.

There is no arithmetic canon from this run. The schema passed multiplication, sum, gcd, and exponent. The facts the workshop trained on are three strings about zero. The planned workshop has no last round, so the stop is a decision, not the exhaustion of a shape.
