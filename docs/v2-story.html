map02

The question

Can random weights, inside a closed term language, leave a shelf someone else can use?

v1 asked whether a group curve would separate from solo and from frozen weights on a holdout. Shared memory lives on disk, in the canon, not in the weights.

A large model from the internet already knows arithmetic. That measurement would be recall, not construction. Downloading weights was out of scope.

Admission, v1

A definition enters only if the breaker does not kill it, 16 audit inputs agree with the hidden target, and the user calls the new slot in a solution that itself passes the audit.

v1 thesis03

v1 protocol

Author, breaker, user, outsider. Then one step.

  • Roles rotate by one after each round. Round 0, seeds 0-3: author 0, breaker 1, user 2, outsider 3.
  • Sampler is uniform over legal productions, 64-token cap. First rounds run at epsilon = 1.
  • Author body may use zero, succ, pred, if0, var, self, and call only to slots already in the canon. No (num k) in a body.
  • Solo: one network, 4 author proposals, admission after 16 audit inputs, 4 learning steps, no breaker.
  • Frozen weights: outsider architecture, seed stored at the start, step never called.
  • Canon cap 32. After 12 rounds without a call the slot returns to the pool. Every 4 rounds the oldest entry is reduced on its audit pairs.

Why v2

The reducer searches shapes. The round number enters the seed.

  • Two round numbers give two task seeds and two breaker-message seeds. v1 task_seed ignored the round. Its breaker used seed + 20.
  • At epsilon = 1, 20 v2 rounds are not all the same author target. Rounds 0..39 contain at least one (break ...).
  • The schema is a finite list: addition, bounded subtraction, multiplication, comparison, parity, bounded sum, gcd, exponent, then equality and tactic facts.
  • During the schema search the network does not choose the term. The utterance is the exercise, one per package after a floor has closed.
  • One command stays in one process: PYTHONPATH=src python -m kanon.v2 --seed 0. A GPU is not required.

R2 is many hours08

Workshop

Levels 0-3 compose cards. Levels 4-6 ask for a tactic.

  • Levels 0-3 are deeper compositions of two cards on the shelf.
  • Levels 4-6: the window holds the claim, the speaker utters a tactic, the kernel grades it.
  • The plan had levels 4-6, and the v2.1 prompts said not to build them yet. The run reached level 4 with an empty task and the steps stopped.
  • Only after tactic tasks were added does the workshop on 4-6 actually train.
  • The workshop has no last round. The stop is a decision.

Clocks

A new level starts at epsilon 1. Author descends by 0.995 to floor 0.25. Executor descends by 0.995 to floor 0.05 only from their first accepted answer on that level. At epsilon = 1 the sampler speaks a legal composition and the network is not called. Below 1 the network starts from the 176-token prefix.

What v2.1 tied together

Utterance and AdamW step share one process.

  • Before v2.1 the exercise in that command said a constant (abstain f0) and the AdamW step did not run.
  • A group hit calls AdamW on seeds 0-3. Solo only on seed 5. Seed 4 is frozen: a sampler attempt, no step.
  • A second launch with resume.pt and canon/snapshot.json loads the frontier and the shelf.
  • A miss, an abstain, and a message the kernel rejects stay off the tape. An empty tape does not move weights.
  • A ship every 6 hours publishes nothing. The shipper is a stub. Without KANON_V2_TOKEN, events.jsonl records that the ship did not go out, and the process continues.

The shelf

Six families came down. The deep facts did not.

Families: constant 0, identity, constant 1, successor, addition, subtraction, parity.

Three shared facts, all shallow:

  • E: (zero) equals (call e4 (zero) (zero)), zero and adding zero to zero.
  • W: (call e0 (zero)) rewrites to (zero), the constant zero.
  • C: the same equality as E, as a short chain.

What the run is not

There is no arithmetic canon from this run.

  • The schema passed multiplication, sum, gcd, and exponent. The facts the workshop trained on are three strings about zero.
  • Fifty tasks were planned once the frontier left floor 8. That file was not created. The gap column stayed empty.
  • A gap of 2 and a gap of 20 would still mean six networks. The code has no threshold that starts a fifth network.
  • The planned workshop has no last round, so the stop is a decision, not the exhaustion of a shape.
  • v1's 1218-event read did not support a second breaker. v2 did not revive that agent.
